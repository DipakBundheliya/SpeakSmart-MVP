## MIC Flow

Question speaks (TTS)
  ↓
Model loads (1-2s first time, instant after)
  ↓ "Listening…" (pulsing mic)
You start speaking (≥ 384ms of voice)
  ↓ Turns red, timer starts
You stop speaking
  ↓ ~1.15s of silence counted
Auto-stops → encodes WAV → sends to Groq

VAD ONNX Model information:
silero_vad_legacy.onnx
Made by the Silero team (Russian ML company)
Very lightweight — ~1.8MB, runs in <1ms per audio frame
Trained on 100+ languages including English
